Why greenhouse seedlings

A better start in the field

🌱

Healthier start

Raised in clean growing media in trays, away from soil-borne pests and diseases of open nursery beds.

📏

Uniform crop

Even, well-rooted seedlings establish together, so your field grows and matures more evenly.

⏱️

Saves you time

Skip weeks of nursery work. Collect transplant-ready seedlings and plant straight away.

🌦️

Climate-smart

Protected from heavy rain, hail and dry spells, with irrigation that uses water carefully.
